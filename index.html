Abstract

Healthcare organizations are deploying artificial intelligence at societal scale across clinical decision support, diagnostics, operations, and population health yet an estimated 70–90% of digital health initiatives fail to deliver expected value (Topol, 2019). These failures are rarely purely technical. They reflect leadership that optimizes algorithms without jointly optimizing the human systems, trust, professional identity, ethical norms, and workforce wellbeing, on which responsible AI depends. In resource-constrained settings, frugal and societally accountable AI requires leaders who steward technology without sacrificing human-centered care.
